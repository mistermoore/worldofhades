# World of Hades — Combat Lab

Two-encounter combat and reward prototype for the [product requirements](docs/PRD.md). Godot 4.6.3, GDScript, Compatibility renderer, single-threaded WebAssembly export. No accounts or external assets required.

## Play

Open `project.godot` in Godot 4.6.3 and run the project, or build and serve the browser export:

```sh
scripts/dev.sh test
scripts/dev.sh build
scripts/dev.sh serve
```

The server listens on port 8000. Serve the exported folder over HTTP; opening the HTML directly does not work. WebGL 2 is required. Install matching Godot web export templates before building. Cloud paths for writable Godot data and caches are configured in `scripts/dev.sh`.

- Space: begin or resume. P or Escape: pause. Enemies wait while paused.
- W/S: forward/back. Q/E: strafe left/right without turning. A/D: turn left/right, with the camera following. Hold right mouse and drag: camera orbit (cursor captured until release). Hold both mouse buttons: move forward while steering the camera. Release either to stop mouse-driven movement. Scroll: zoom. C: reset the camera to its closer right-shoulder view.
- Tab: cycle living targets.
- Strike automatically attacks the selected living enemy once per second in melee range. Tab switches the automatic attack to your new target. It waits out of range without chasing, and stops when the selected enemy dies until you select another. Cleave, Charge, and the reward on 4 take priority on the next shared cooldown. Pause suspends attacks. No activation key is needed.
- 1 — Strike: 18 damage, generates 15 Momentum, 2.8m range.
- 2 — Cleave: 38 damage in a frontal cone, costs 30 Momentum, 4s cooldown.
- 3 — Charge: 12 damage, closes distance and interrupts, 13m range, 8s cooldown.
- R or Restart: return to the ready screen with a fresh encounter. All abilities trigger a 1s global cooldown.

The opening is staggered: Ashguard immediately, Ember Seer after 10 seconds, and Cinder Acolyte after 18 seconds. Each defeated enemy restores up to 20 health.

The Ashguard pursues and telegraphs a frontal attack with a filled cone: move outside it or behind the enemy. Enemy casts give at least 2.4 seconds to respond. A banner warns when you are standing in danger. Health/Momentum bars, target distance, floating damage/healing, and a hit flash show what is happening.

The Ember Seer marks your location before an area blast: leave the circle or interrupt. The Cinder Acolyte heals surviving enemies: prioritize or interrupt it. Clear all three to win; zero health ends the encounter.

## Architecture

`scripts/combat.gd` is a rendering-independent, command-driven simulation. Ability data, resource rules, targeting, enemy decisions, and outcome resolution live there. `scripts/arena.gd` owns input, procedural 3D scenery, camera, and HUD. Simulation tests run without a renderer.

Godot is selected over Bevy for this milestone because its integrated 3D editor, camera/UI tooling, and GDScript iteration favor a solo developer. Bevy offers stronger Rust/ECS foundations but requires more integration for this small first encounter. This prototype does not yet validate animation workflows or multiplayer latency; reconsider the choice if those tests expose constraints.

## Scope and next work

This is a solo, greybox combat prototype. Multiplayer, a larger upgrade pool, complete dungeons, animation assets, audio, and performance targets remain unimplemented/unmeasured. Geometric characters deliberately keep the initial implementation independent of art assets. The next milestone should playtest combat feel, then introduce authoritative networking before expanding content.

See `docs/PROTOTYPE.md` for decisions and validation.

## Hosted demo

Play at **https://mistermoore.github.io/worldofhades/** in a desktop browser with WebGL 2.

Pushing to `main` runs `.github/workflows/pages.yml`: it installs checksum-verified Godot 4.6.3, runs combat tests, exports the release browser build, and deploys to GitHub Pages. The workflow can also be started manually from GitHub Actions. The public demo is solo only; no accounts or backend services are needed. Check the workflow's deployment result before expecting an update to appear.

Deployed game assets include the commit SHA in their filenames to keep browser caches from mixing old and new releases. A small build ID appears at the bottom right. If an already-open tab shows an older demo, reload it; use the current build ID to confirm the update. The stable entry page remains `index.html`.

## First reward loop

Clear Trial 1 to choose one active reward. Click a card, then press Space to start Trial 2. The selected ability appears on **4** and uses Momentum generated by Strike (manual or automatic):

- **Second Wind:** heal 45 health, 20 Momentum, 8s cooldown. Cannot be wasted at full health.
- **Lightning Bolt:** 45 damage at up to 14m, 25 Momentum, 6s cooldown.
- **Rending Slash:** 18 melee damage plus 30 bleed damage over 5s, 20 Momentum, 5s cooldown. Reapplication refreshes bleed rather than stacking it.

Choosing a reward restores up to 40 health and starts Trial 2 with 30 Momentum. Enemies have 25% more health in Trial 2. Clear it to complete the run; R resets everything so you can try another reward. Rewards last only for this two-encounter prototype run.

Charge interrupting a cast exposes that enemy for 3 seconds, increasing damage taken by 25%. Look for the interrupt burst and EXPOSED label. The healer shows green beams while casting; the caster marks its blast with an orange orb and ground pulse. These effects supplement the existing danger zones.
